Web & Software Development Services

Do you require a highly functional, well designed portfolio?

My strength lies in my creative and collaborative approach to web development. I am both a resilient and a customer-first problem-solver, which means the more "unrealistic" your vision might be, the more driven to make that vision possible for you. Both you and I know your website is a communication tool - that allows you to sell your services and market yourself - and a strong, secure digital platform helps you do that while futureproofing you for the ever-evolving information age.

For transparency, I do not build server-side applications. I am also not accredited to issue formal IT advice, or equipped to build software that handles sensitive data. If you require more professional software development services, it may be in your best interest to look elsewhere.

3D Logo and Asset Designs

I use Blender - an open source 3D design software known for its complexity and versatility - to build 3D Visuals, Logos, and Digital Twins. I've built a number of assets that are already usable in Unity, WebGL, and Unreal Engine 5. If you need a logo, a 3D Visual, or a Digital Twin designed - I'd be happy to help.

To be clear, my professional expertise is limited to the following above. I don't do sculpting or rigging and cannot build video game characters. If you're looking for this, a more professional 3D studio may be a better choice.
